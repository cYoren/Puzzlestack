Letter Cancel

Find all "" letters

Play Letter CancelRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap on the tiles containing the target letter to select them.

  1. Identify the target letter you need to find at the start.
  2. Scan the grid carefully for the target letter.
  3. Tap each correct letter.
  4. Find all instances to complete the grid.

Strategy

  • Scan row by row or column by column systematically to ensure you don'''t miss any.
  • Ignore similar-looking letters that are placed as distractors.

What it asks of you

Find and select all instances of the target letter from the grid while ignoring distractions.
