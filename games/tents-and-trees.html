Tents & Trees

Place tents next to trees. Numbers indicate how many tents are in each row/column. Tents cannot touch each other.

Play Tents & TreesRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap an empty cell to place a tent, tap again to cycle to grass (empty), or tap again to clear.

  1. Look at the row and column clues indicating how many tents belong in each line.
  2. Find trees and tap adjacent empty cells to place tents next to them.
  3. Ensure no two tents touch each other, even diagonally.
  4. Complete the grid so all trees have a tent and all clues are satisfied.

Strategy

  • Fill rows or columns with a '0' clue entirely with grass.
  • If a tree only has one valid empty space next to it, a tent must go there.

What it asks of you

Place tents in the grid so that every tree is paired with exactly one adjacent tent, no two tents touch each other (even diagonally), and the tent counts match the row and column clues.
