Kakuro

Solve crosswords with numbers and sums.

Play KakuroRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap a white cell to select it, then tap a number on the keypad to enter it. Use the backspace button to clear a cell.

  1. Find a clue block (number with a diagonal line).
  2. The top-right number is the target sum for the horizontal block to its right. The bottom-left is for the vertical block below it.
  3. Tap a white cell and use the keypad to enter a digit from 1 to 9.
  4. Ensure no digits repeat within a single sum block.

Strategy

  • Learn common Kakuro combinations (e.g., a 2-cell sum of 3 must be 1+2; a 2-cell sum of 4 must be 1+3).
  • Look for intersections of restrictive sums (e.g., a low horizontal sum crossing a low vertical sum).
  • Use pencil marks or mental notes to track possible candidates for each cell.

What it asks of you

Fill the white cells with digits 1-9 so that the sum of each contiguous horizontal/vertical block matches its clue, without repeating any digit within a block.

Questions

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Kakuro take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Kakuro?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Kakuro practice?
