Klotski Escape

Slide blocks to reach the exits.

Play Klotski EscapeRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap a block to select it, then tap the directional arrows that appear on it, or swipe the block to move it into an empty space.

  1. Identify the 2x2 HERO block.
  2. Move the smaller blocks around to clear a path downward.
  3. Continue sliding blocks into empty spaces without overlapping.
  4. Reach the green escape zone at the bottom middle with the HERO block to win.

Strategy

  • Focus on moving the smaller 1x1 blocks into empty spaces to free up larger ones.
  • Try to keep the empty spaces together to allow the large 2x2 block to move.

What it asks of you

Move the 2x2 HERO block to the escape zone at the bottom middle of the board by sliding blocks out of the way.
