Color Maze Nav

Navigate maze tiles matching dynamic permission colors.

Play Color Maze NavRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Swipe to move your character to an adjacent valid tile.

  1. Observe the required color sequence shown at the top.
  2. Move from the start tile to the next adjacent tile in the sequence.
  3. Continue looping through the sequence as you move.
  4. Reach the exit without breaking the pattern.

Strategy

  • Always double-check what the next required color is.
  • Plan your path a few steps ahead to avoid dead ends.
  • If you get stuck, retrace your steps carefully.

What it asks of you

Navigate the maze by following a specific sequence of colored tiles.
