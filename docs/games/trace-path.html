Trace Path

Follow the target path as closely as you can

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Tap and hold on the starting marker, then drag your finger along the path to the ending marker.

  1. Locate the starting marker of the target path.
  2. Press and hold your finger on the start marker.
  3. Drag your finger along the curve of the path, trying to stay exactly on the line.
  4. Release your finger when you reach the end marker to get your accuracy score.

Strategy

  • Move at a steady, moderate pace; moving too fast reduces accuracy, but moving too slow makes it hard to draw smooth curves.
  • Look slightly ahead of your finger to anticipate the curves.

What it asks of you

Carefully trace the drawn path from the start point to the end point, staying as close to the line as possible to achieve high accuracy.

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Trace Path?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
