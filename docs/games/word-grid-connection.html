Word Connections

Group 16 words into four categories of four.

Play Word ConnectionsRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap to select or deselect exactly 4 words from the grid. Once 4 are selected, tap the 'Submit Group' button that appears at the bottom.

  1. Scan the 16 scrambled words in the grid to find potential connections or themes.
  2. Tap on exactly 4 words that you believe belong to the same category.
  3. Tap the 'Submit Group' button that appears at the bottom.
  4. If correct, the category is solved and removed from the grid. If incorrect, you lose a try. Solve all 4 categories before running out of tries to win.

Strategy

  • Don't submit immediately if you see a connection; make sure there aren't 5 or 6 words that fit the category, which means you need to solve another overlapping category first.
  • If you're stuck, try finding the most unique or specific word on the board and figure out what it must connect to.

What it asks of you

The objective is to group a 16-word grid into four categories of four related words each, within a limited number of tries.

Questions

Is Word Connections free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Word Connections take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Word Connections?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Word Connections practice?

It is a word puzzle: the objective is to group a 16-word grid into four categories of four related words each, within a limited number of tries.
