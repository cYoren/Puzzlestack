Spatial Cabinets

Memorize and locate items behind cabinet doors.

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Tap the compartments to reveal or place objects based on your memory.

  1. Observe the cabinet as doors open to reveal objects inside.
  2. Memorize which object belongs in which compartment.
  3. When the doors close, you will be prompted to find specific objects.
  4. Tap the correct compartment to reveal the requested object.

Strategy

  • Group objects together by rows or columns in your mind.
  • Create a mental map or a story connecting adjacent items.
  • Focus on the general layout rather than staring at one single item.

What it asks of you

Memorize the locations of various objects within a grid or cabinet and recall them accurately.

Do I have to guess in Spatial Cabinets?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
