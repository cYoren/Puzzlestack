Venn Sets

Select the corresponding regions of the Venn diagram for the set formula.

Play Venn SetsRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap regions of the Venn diagram or the outside space to select them, then tap SUBMIT.

  1. Read the set expression (e.g., A ∩ B, A ∪ B, A \ B, (A ∪ B)').
  2. Identify whether the formula targets only A, only B, the intersection, or the outer universe.
  3. Toggle the appropriate region cards and submit your answer.

Strategy

  • Intersection (∩) means only the overlapping region.
  • Union (∪) combines all interior parts of both sets.
  • Complement (') refers to everything outside the specified set.

What it asks of you

Select the correct regions of the Venn diagram matching the given set theory formula.
