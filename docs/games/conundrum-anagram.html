Conundrum

Unscramble all 9 letters to discover the hidden full dictionary word.

Play ConundrumRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap letter tiles in order to spell the word; tap BACKSPACE to undo.

  1. Scan the scrambled 9-letter pool.
  2. Look for common prefixes (DIS-, UN-) and suffixes (-ING, -TION, -FUL).
  3. Spell out the complete 9-letter word.

Strategy

  • Group consonants with matching vowel sounds.
  • Try constructing the ending first to reveal the root stem.

What it asks of you

Unscramble all 9 letter tiles into a single valid English word.

Questions

Is Conundrum free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Conundrum take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Conundrum?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Conundrum practice?

It is a word puzzle: unscramble all 9 letter tiles into a single valid English word.
