Frequency Tap

Tap the button matching the target light pulse frequency.

Play Frequency TapRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the left or right panel that corresponds to the correct frequency.

  1. Read the target frequency at the top of the screen.
  2. Observe the pulsing speeds of the 'A' and 'B' panels.
  3. Tap the panel that pulses at the target frequency.
  4. Answer correctly to increase your score before time runs out.

Strategy

  • Use the reference frequency as a baseline (e.g., 1.0Hz is one pulse per second).
  • Higher frequencies pulse faster; lower frequencies pulse slower.
  • Rely on the visual rhythm rather than counting exact milliseconds.

What it asks of you

Compare the pulse rates of two light panels and identify which one matches the target frequency (e.g., 1.5Hz).
