Slitherlink

Loop the pin grid using number clues.

Play SlitherlinkRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the edges between dots to draw or remove a line.

  1. Look at the numbers inside the cells.
  2. The number indicates exactly how many of its 4 edges are part of the loop.
  3. Draw lines to satisfy all numbers.
  4. Ensure the lines form exactly one closed loop.

Strategy

  • A '0' means no lines can surround it; mark them with 'x' to visually eliminate them.
  • Adjacent '3's always have parallel lines on their outer edges and a line between them.
  • The loop cannot branch or intersect itself, which helps logically force paths.

What it asks of you

Connect adjacent dots to form a single continuous loop without crossings or branches.

Do I have to guess in Slitherlink?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
