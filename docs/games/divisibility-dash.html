Divisibility Dash

Tap all single-digit divisors (2-9) for the given number.

Play Divisibility DashRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the falling or appearing numbers that are cleanly divisible by the target.

  1. Note the target divisor shown at the top of the screen.
  2. Watch numbers appear on the board.
  3. Quickly perform mental division.
  4. Tap only the numbers that divide evenly by the target.

Strategy

  • Memorize divisibility rules (e.g., ends in 0/5 for 5, sum of digits for 3 and 9).
  • Ignore obvious wrong answers (e.g., odd numbers for an even divisor).
  • Focus on accuracy first, speed will follow.

What it asks of you

Identify numbers that are divisible by a given target number within a time limit.

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
