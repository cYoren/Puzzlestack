Mental Rotation

Are these shapes the same, just rotated?

Play Mental RotationRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap 'Match' or 'Different' based on your spatial visualization.

  1. Look at the primary 3D block structure.
  2. Look at the second 3D block structure.
  3. Mentally rotate the first structure to align with the second.
  4. Decide if they are the exact same shape or a mirrored version.

Strategy

  • Focus on a single distinctive feature or 'arm' of the shape and track its rotation.
  • Try to identify the handedness (left/right) of asymmetric parts.

What it asks of you

Determine if two 3D shapes are identical but rotated, or mirror images.
