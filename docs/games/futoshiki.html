Futoshiki

Fill grids with greater-than clues.

Play FutoshikiRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap a cell to select it, then use the numpad at the bottom to enter a number. Use the erase button to clear a cell.

  1. Look for inequality signs between adjacent cells.
  2. Find rows or columns with missing numbers and use the signs to deduce the values.
  3. Tap an empty cell and select the correct number from the bottom row.
  4. Complete the grid without violating any inequality constraints or row/column uniqueness.

Strategy

  • Look for chains of inequalities (e.g., A < B < C) which severely restrict possible numbers.
  • Identify the highest and lowest possible numbers in the grid to fill edges of inequalities.
  • Use the process of elimination in rows and columns as you would in Sudoku.

What it asks of you

Fill the grid with numbers such that each row and column contains unique numbers, while respecting the inequality signs (<, >) between cells.

Questions

Is Futoshiki free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Futoshiki take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Futoshiki?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Futoshiki practice?

It is a logic puzzle: fill the grid with numbers such that each row and column contains unique numbers, while respecting the inequality signs (<, >) between cells.
