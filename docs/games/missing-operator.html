Operator Mix

Fill the blanks to complete the equation

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Tap an empty operator slot, then tap the desired operator (+, -, ×, ÷).

  1. Analyze the given numbers and the target result of the equation.
  2. Estimate which operations will get you closer to the target.
  3. Tap the empty slots and insert operators.
  4. The game checks if your filled equation evaluates to the target result.

Strategy

  • Remember the order of operations (multiplication/division before addition/subtraction).
  • If the target is much larger than the operands, you likely need multiplication.
  • If the target is small or fractional, look for division or subtraction.

What it asks of you

Fill in the missing mathematical operators to make the given equation correct.

Questions

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Operator Mix take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Operator Mix?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Operator Mix practice?

It is a math puzzle: fill in the missing mathematical operators to make the given equation correct.
