Compound Connect

Combine two words to form a compound word

Play Compound ConnectRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap a word from the first list, then tap a word from the second list.

  1. Look at the provided word fragments.
  2. Identify pairs that combine to make a real compound word.
  3. Select the first half, then the second half.
  4. Clear all words from the board.

Strategy

  • Start with the most obvious pairs first.
  • If a word doesn't fit, think about alternative meanings.
  • Read the remaining words aloud to see if they sound right together.

What it asks of you

Connect two words to form a valid compound word.

Questions

Is Compound Connect free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Compound Connect take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Compound Connect?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Compound Connect practice?

It is a word puzzle: connect two words to form a valid compound word.
