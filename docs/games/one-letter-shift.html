One-Letter Shift

Transform one word into another with one letter change

Play One-Letter ShiftRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Use the keyboard to type the next valid word in the sequence.

  1. Look at your starting word and the target final word.
  2. Identify a single letter you can change to form a new, valid dictionary word.
  3. Input the new word.
  4. Repeat the process until you successfully reach the target word.

Strategy

  • Work from both ends; think of words that are one letter away from the target.
  • Vowels are usually the easiest to swap out to make new words.
  • Don't worry if your path isn't the shortest, as long as every step is a real word.

What it asks of you

Transform one word into another by changing exactly one letter at a time, creating valid words at each step.

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in One-Letter Shift?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
