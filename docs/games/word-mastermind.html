Word Mastermind

Crack the 4-letter code! Bulls (B) are perfect spots, Cows (C) are wrong spots.

Play Word MastermindRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Use the on-screen keyboard to type a 4-letter guess and press ENTER to submit.

  1. Enter a 4-letter starting word using the keyboard and press ENTER.
  2. Review the feedback provided next to your guess: 'B' indicates a Bull (correct letter in the correct position), and 'C' indicates a Cow (correct letter but in the wrong position).
  3. Use this logic to refine your next guess.
  4. Keep guessing until you get 4 Bulls, revealing the target word before you run out of attempts.

Strategy

  • Use a starting word with 4 unique common letters (like 'TEAR' or 'LION') to maximize initial information.
  • If you get a 'Cow', try moving that letter to a different position in your next guess.

What it asks of you

The objective is to guess the hidden 4-letter target word by entering guesses and using the Bulls (B) and Cows (C) feedback.

Do I have to guess in Word Mastermind?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
