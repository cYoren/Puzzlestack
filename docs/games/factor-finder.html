Factor Finder

Identify all factors of target numbers.

Play Factor FinderRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the correct number bubbles that are factors of the target.

  1. Look at the large target number displayed on the screen.
  2. Review the floating or grid-based number options.
  3. Tap all the numbers that are valid factors (divide evenly into the target).
  4. Avoid tapping numbers that are not factors to prevent losing points.

Strategy

  • Start with the easiest factors: 2 (if even), 3 (if sum of digits is divisible by 3), and 5.
  • Remember that factors often come in pairs (e.g., if 2 is a factor of 24, so is 12).
  • Work systematically from smallest to largest.

What it asks of you

Identify all the prime factors or regular factors of a given target number.

Questions

Is Factor Finder free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Factor Finder take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Factor Finder?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Factor Finder practice?

It is a math puzzle: identify all the prime factors or regular factors of a given target number.
