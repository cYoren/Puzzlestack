Word Wheel

Build words of 3+ letters using the centre letter.

Play Word WheelRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the letter buttons to spell a word, use the backspace button to correct mistakes, and tap the SUBMIT button to enter your guess.

  1. Look at the letter wheel, paying special attention to the central letter.
  2. Spell out a word using the provided letters (minimum 3 letters) by tapping the letter buttons. The word MUST contain the central letter.
  3. Tap SUBMIT to check your word. If it's valid, it will be added to your found words list.
  4. Find the required target number of words to win the game.

Strategy

  • Try finding prefixes and suffixes you can make with the outer letters to attach to the central letter.
  • Look for the 'Pangram' - a word that uses all 7 letters provided in the wheel.

What it asks of you

The objective is to find valid words using the provided letters, where each word must include the central letter.

Questions

Is Word Wheel free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and no personal data collected. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Word Wheel take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Word Wheel?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Word Wheel practice?

It is a word puzzle: the objective is to find valid words using the provided letters, where each word must include the central letter.
