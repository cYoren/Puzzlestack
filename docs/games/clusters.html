Clusters

Fill the grid with two colours. A dot marks a cell with exactly one neighbour of its colour; every other cell needs two.

Play ClustersRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap a cell to cycle it: empty, then one colour, then the other, then empty again. A dotted cell is fixed and does not change.

  1. Read a dot as a small puzzle of its own. The dot already shows the colour of its cell, so that cell is done, and exactly one of its neighbours shares it.
  2. A corner cell without a dot has only two neighbours, so both of them must be its colour. That is the strongest start on the board.
  3. Count neighbours, not patterns. A cell needs two of its colour, so when one neighbour is the other colour and one is empty, the empty one is decided.
  4. A dotted cell with no same-coloured neighbour yet needs its one from the cells still open. When only one is open, that one takes the colour.
  5. The board is finished when every cell is coloured, every dot has exactly one neighbour of its colour, and every plain cell has two. A cell that cannot reach its count shows in red.

Strategy

  • Work from the corners inward: a corner without a dot forces both of its neighbours, which then forces the next ring.
  • A dot with one neighbour already its colour rules out that colour for every other neighbour, which is often the fastest way to place an edge.
  • Two colours are symmetrical, so a mistake is not local: a cell that cannot reach two neighbours of its colour usually means the cell two steps away is wrong.
  • An edge cell has three neighbours, so it needs two of its own colour: one of the three is the odd one out, and the dot tells you which.

What it asks of you

Colour every cell one of two colours, so that a dotted cell has exactly one neighbour of its colour and a cell without a dot has at least two.

Questions

Is Clusters free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Clusters take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Clusters?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Clusters practice?

It is a logic puzzle: colour every cell one of two colours, so that a dotted cell has exactly one neighbour of its colour and a cell without a dot has at least two.
