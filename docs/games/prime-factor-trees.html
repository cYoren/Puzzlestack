Prime Factor Trees

Decompose composite numbers into prime leaf factor pairs.

Play Prime Factor TreesRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap empty nodes and select the correct prime factors.

  1. Look at the main number at the top of the tree.
  2. Find two numbers that multiply to make that number.
  3. Continue breaking down composite numbers until only primes remain.
  4. Fill in all nodes correctly to finish the tree.

Strategy

  • Always check if a number is divisible by 2 or 3 first.
  • Remember that branches must multiply to equal the node above them.
  • Only prime numbers belong at the very bottom leaf nodes.

What it asks of you

Break down a composite number into its prime factors by completing a branching tree diagram.
