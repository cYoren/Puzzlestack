Balance scale

Deduce the weight of the last item based on the scales.

Play Balance scaleRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Use the virtual number pad to type your calculated answer, then tap the checkmark submit button.

  1. Review the balance scales displayed on the screen. Each scale represents a mathematical equation.
  2. Identify the known weight values (usually represented by standard numbers or shapes you can calculate).
  3. Use substitution to find the weight of the unknown items on the scales.
  4. Determine the weight of the final target item marked with a question mark.
  5. Enter your deduced answer using the number pad.

Strategy

  • Find a scale where identical items balance a known number to quickly establish a base value.
  • Treat the scales exactly like algebraic equations. If 2 items balance 10, then 1 item is 5.
  • Work methodically from the simplest scale to the most complex one.

What it asks of you

Deduce the numerical weight of a target item based on visual balance scale equations.
