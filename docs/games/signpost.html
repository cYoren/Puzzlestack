Signpost

Give every cell the next number, following the arrows.

Play SignpostRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Drag a cell onto the cell it points at, or tap one cell and then the other. An arrow turns solid once its cell points somewhere; tapping that cell again takes the link back.

  1. Start from the given numbers. A given pins down the cell it sits in, and often the arrow into it.
  2. Follow each arrow as far as it can go along its line. The arrow runs along the whole ray, so the cell it means can be any distance away.
  3. An arrow that has only one cell left in its ray is decided for you: link it.
  4. Finish when one chain runs from 1 to the last number with every arrow pointing the way.

Strategy

  • An arrow never points at a neighbour by default: it points at whatever lies along its line, so look to the end of the ray first.
  • Work backwards from the highest given number as well as forwards; both ends are constrained.
  • Every cell is used exactly once, so a number you cannot place in one ray is a reason to re-read the others.

What it asks of you

Number every cell from 1 to the last so that each cell points at the cell holding the next number.

Questions

Is Signpost free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Signpost take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Signpost?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Signpost practice?

It is a logic puzzle: number every cell from 1 to the last so that each cell points at the cell holding the next number.
