Negative Space

Identify the shape formed by the empty space between objects.

Play Negative SpaceRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the correct answer from the options, or type the identified shape.

  1. Look at the complex image presented on the screen.
  2. Shift your focus from the solid objects to the empty background spaces.
  3. Identify the cohesive shape formed by that empty space.
  4. Select or input the correct answer.

Strategy

  • Try squinting or relaxing your eyes to blur the foreground.
  • Look at the edges of the solid objects; they form the outline of the negative shape.
  • Mentally invert the colors (imagine the background is black and objects are white).

What it asks of you

Identify the hidden shape or letter formed by the negative space between objects.

Questions

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Negative Space?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
