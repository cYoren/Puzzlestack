Block Count 3D

Determine the total number of individual cubes that make up a complex 3D block structure.

Play Block Count 3DRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Drag your finger across the 3D viewport to rotate the structure. Use the virtual numpad to enter your count and tap submit.

  1. Examine the 3D block structure displayed on the screen.
  2. Drag to rotate the model and view it from different angles to ensure you don'''t miss hidden blocks.
  3. Count the blocks, remembering that any block on a higher tier must be supported by blocks beneath it.
  4. Type the total number of blocks using the virtual numpad.
  5. Tap the submit button to check your answer.

Strategy

  • Count column by column. A column that is 3 blocks high contributes 3 to your total.
  • Rotate the view to look straight down from the top to easily count the base footprint.
  • Ensure you account for blocks completely hidden in the center of a larger structure.

What it asks of you

Determine the total number of individual cubes that make up a complex 3D block structure.
