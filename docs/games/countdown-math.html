Countdown Math

Combine random numbers to calculate the three-digit target.

Play Countdown MathRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap numbers and operators to build your equation.

  1. You are given a target number and a set of starting numbers.
  2. Select a number, then an operator, then another number.
  3. The result becomes a new available number.
  4. Continue combining until you reach the exact target.

Strategy

  • Work backwards from the target number if possible.
  • Save small numbers for fine-tuning at the end.
  • Don'''t be afraid to clear and start over if you get stuck.

What it asks of you

Use the provided numbers and basic arithmetic operators to reach a target number before time runs out.

Questions

Is Countdown Math free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Countdown Math take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Countdown Math?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Countdown Math practice?

It is a math puzzle: use the provided numbers and basic arithmetic operators to reach a target number before time runs out.
