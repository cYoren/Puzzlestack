Oxymoron Hunt

Pair contradictory words in the grid

Play Oxymoron HuntRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the phrase that constitutes an oxymoron.

  1. Read through all the provided phrases carefully.
  2. Look for phrases where two consecutive or closely placed words have opposite meanings.
  3. Discard normal descriptive phrases.
  4. Tap the contradictory phrase (e.g., 'deafening silence') to score.

Strategy

  • Look for extreme adjectives paired with extreme nouns.
  • Read the literal meaning of each word independently to spot the contradiction.
  • Familiarize yourself with common oxymorons like 'jumbo shrimp' or 'awfully good'.

What it asks of you

Identify the oxymoron (a phrase containing contradictory terms) from a list of phrases.
