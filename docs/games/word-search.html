Word Search

Find all the hidden words in the grid. Drag to select.

Play Word SearchRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Drag your finger across the letters in the grid (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) to highlight and select a word.

  1. Review the 'WORDS TO FIND' list at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Scan the letter grid above to locate the first letter of one of the words.
  3. Once found, press and drag your finger from the first letter to the last letter of the word, then release.
  4. Find and highlight all the words in the list to complete the puzzle.

Strategy

  • Search for the least common letters in a word (like Q, X, Z, K, or V) rather than starting with the first letter.
  • Scan row by row or column by column systematically if you're stuck.

What it asks of you

The objective is to find all the listed words hidden within the grid of letters.

Questions

Is Word Search free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Word Search take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Word Search?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Word Search practice?

It is a word puzzle: the objective is to find all the listed words hidden within the grid of letters.
