Topology

Are these shapes topologically equivalent? (Can one be deformed into the other without cutting or gluing?)

Play TopologyRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the YES button if they are topologically equivalent, or the NO button if they are not.

  1. Look at the two shapes or characters presented on the screen.
  2. Count the number of holes (closed loops) in each shape.
  3. Consider the number of endpoints or intersections.
  4. Tap YES if they have the same topological properties, or NO if they differ.

Strategy

  • The most important feature is the number of holes; an 'O' and a 'D' are equivalent (1 hole), but an 'O' and an '8' are not (1 vs 2 holes).
  • Imagine the shape is made of rubber and see if you can bend one into the other.

What it asks of you

Determine if two displayed characters or shapes are topologically equivalent, meaning one can be stretched or deformed into the other without tearing or merging holes.
