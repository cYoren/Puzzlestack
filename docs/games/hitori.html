Hitori

Shade duplicate numbers without splitting the grid.

Play HitoriRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap a cell once to shade (black), tap twice to circle (safe).

  1. Locate rows and columns with duplicate numbers.
  2. Shade duplicates so only one instance remains visible.
  3. Ensure shaded cells are never orthogonally adjacent.

Strategy

  • If a cell is shaded black, all four orthogonal neighbors must be unshaded (circle them).
  • Between two identical numbers with one cell in between, the middle cell must be unshaded.

What it asks of you

Shade duplicate numbers in the grid such that no row or column contains repeats among unshaded cells, black cells don't touch orthogonally, and unshaded cells remain connected.
