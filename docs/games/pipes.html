Pipes

Connect matching colored dots with pipes.

Play PipesRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap on individual pipe tiles to rotate them 90 degrees clockwise.

  1. Identify the start and end points of the pipeline.
  2. Tap pieces to align them towards adjacent tiles.
  3. Connect all segments ensuring there are no open, leaking ends.
  4. Complete the connection to let the fluid flow and finish the level.

Strategy

  • Start from the edges or fixed points and work your way inwards.
  • Look for corners and straight pieces that can only fit in one logical way.
  • Avoid creating closed loops that don't connect to the main line.

What it asks of you

Rotate pipe segments to create a continuous, unbroken flow from the start point to the end point.

Questions

Is Pipes free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Pipes take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Pipes?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Pipes practice?

It is a logic puzzle: rotate pipe segments to create a continuous, unbroken flow from the start point to the end point.
