Sequence

Repeat shape sequences from memory.

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Tap the colored buttons in the correct order.

  1. Watch the buttons flash in a specific sequence.
  2. Wait for the sequence to finish playing.
  3. Repeat the sequence by tapping the buttons in the exact same order.
  4. With each round, a new step is added to the sequence.

Strategy

  • Use both visual and auditory memory; hum the tune while visualizing the colors.
  • Assign numbers or words to the colors and repeat them in your head.
  • Group the sequence into chunks of 3 or 4 to make it easier to remember.

Questions

Is Sequence free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Sequence take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Sequence?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Sequence practice?

It is a memory puzzle: mEMORY
