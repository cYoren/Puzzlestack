Cross-Section Slice

Identify the correct 2D cross-section of a 3D object from a specific angle.

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Tap the button corresponding to the correct 2D shape at the bottom of the screen.

  1. Observe the 3D solid and the highlighted plane slicing through it in the main view area.
  2. Mentally visualize the 2D cross-section shape created exactly where the plane intersects the solid.
  3. Look at the 2D shape options at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Tap the option that matches your visualized shape. Correctly identify the target number of cross-sections to win.

Strategy

  • Pay close attention to the angle of the slicing plane; a diagonal cut through a cylinder makes an ellipse, not a circle.
  • Imagine looking directly 'down' the slicing plane perpendicular to its surface to 'see' the true 2D shape.

What it asks of you

The objective is to correctly identify the 2D shape that results from slicing a 3D solid with a plane.
