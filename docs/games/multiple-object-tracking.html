Object Tracker

Watch the highlighted objects. Track them as they move, then select them.

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Tap the objects you believe were the original targets after they stop moving.

  1. Memorize the items that are briefly highlighted at the start.
  2. Keep your eyes focused on those targets as all items begin to move.
  3. Maintain focus until all movement stops.
  4. Tap on the exact items that were originally highlighted.

Strategy

  • Don't look directly at one object; use a soft, central gaze to track the whole group.
  • Group the targets mentally into a polygon and watch how the shape morphs.
  • Avoid getting distracted by items crossing paths.

What it asks of you

Keep track of specific target objects as they move randomly among identical distractors.
