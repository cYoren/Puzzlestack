3D Maze Navigation

Navigate through a 3D maze to find the exit from a first-person perspective.

Play 3D Maze NavigationRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Use the on-screen directional buttons or swipe to move forward, backward, and turn.

  1. Look around to orient yourself in the 3D environment.
  2. Move forward through the corridors.
  3. Memorize intersections to avoid walking in circles.
  4. Find the exit door or portal to win.

Strategy

  • Use the 'right-hand rule': always keep your right hand on the wall to eventually find the exit.
  • Look out for unique textures or landmarks to remember where you've been.

What it asks of you

Navigate through a 3D maze to find the exit from a first-person perspective.
