Rhythm Master

Tap in sync with the pulse

Play Rhythm MasterRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the designated button in sync with the memorized rhythm.

  1. Listen closely to the auditory rhythm played.
  2. Watch visual indicators if available to grasp the timing.
  3. When prompted, tap the screen to replicate the beat.
  4. Maintain the exact tempo and pauses of the original sequence.

Strategy

  • Tap your foot or nod your head to internalize the beat while listening.
  • Subdivide the beat in your head to handle off-beat taps.
  • Don't rush; keeping a steady internal metronome is key.

What it asks of you

Listen to a rhythm sequence and tap it back with accurate timing.
