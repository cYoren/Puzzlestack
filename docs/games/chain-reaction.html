Chain Reaction

Connect words using overlapping letters

Play Chain ReactionRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Use the keyboard to type a word into the text field, then tap the add icon or press submit on the keyboard.

  1. Look at the last word currently at the bottom of the chain.
  2. Identify the last two letters of that word, which are highlighted.
  3. Think of a new valid English word that begins with those exact two letters.
  4. Type the word into the input field and submit it to grow the chain.
  5. Keep the chain going as long as possible before the time expires.

Strategy

  • Keep words relatively short to save typing time, as the score is based on the chain length, not word complexity.
  • Avoid using words that end in rare two-letter combinations (like '''ix''' or '''uz''') which will make your next turn very difficult.
  • If you are stuck, try adding common suffixes like '''er''', '''ed''', or '''ing''' to a base word if it helps set up an easier ending.

What it asks of you

Build the longest word chain possible by entering words that start with the last two letters of the previous word.

Questions

Is Chain Reaction free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Chain Reaction take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Chain Reaction?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Chain Reaction practice?

It is a word puzzle: build the longest word chain possible by entering words that start with the last two letters of the previous word.
