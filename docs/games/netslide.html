Netslide

Slide rows and columns until every pipe meets a pipe.

Play NetslideRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Drag a tile one square along its row or column: the whole line slides round one step. Tiles never rotate. A wall across a line blocks it.

  1. Find the lines that cannot move. A wall stops its line, so those tiles are already where they will be.
  2. Slide a line that a misplaced tile sits in, and watch where the tile goes.
  3. Work pipe by pipe: join one pair, then keep that join out of the line you slide next.
  4. The board is won when every pipe meets a pipe.

Strategy

  • A slide moves a whole line, so every tile in that line changes place at once. Count what moves with it.
  • Slides come in pairs: sliding the same line back restores the board.
  • Leave the line with the fewest tiles until last; it has the least room for mistakes.

What it asks of you

Slide rows and columns until every pipe is joined to a pipe.

Questions

Is Netslide free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Netslide take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Netslide?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
