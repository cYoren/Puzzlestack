Laser Link

Rotate mirrors to reflect lasers and power up all the targets.

Play Laser LinkRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap on empty cells in the grid to cycle through mirror types (forward slash '/' or backslash '\').

  1. Observe the laser source position and its initial direction.
  2. Note the target positions the laser must reach.
  3. Tap grid cells to place mirrors and reflect the laser path.
  4. Ensure the laser hits all targets to complete the level.

Strategy

  • Trace the laser path backward from the targets to figure out where mirrors are needed.
  • Experiment with both mirror types to find the correct reflection angles.

What it asks of you

Place mirrors to reflect the laser from the source so that it hits all the target positions.
