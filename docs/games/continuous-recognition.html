Continuous Recog

Identify if the card presented is old or new.

Play Continuous RecogRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap '''New''' if it's the first time seeing it, or '''Old''' if you've seen it before.

  1. Images will appear on screen one by one.
  2. For each image, decide if it's a new item or a repeat.
  3. Tap the corresponding New or Old button.
  4. Keep track as the sequence gets longer.

Strategy

  • Focus on unique details of each image to remember it better.
  • Trust your gut reaction; overthinking can lead to mistakes.
  • Stay highly focused, as a momentary lapse can ruin your streak.

What it asks of you

Determine if the currently shown image has appeared before in the sequence.

Questions

Is Continuous Recog free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Continuous Recog take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Continuous Recog?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Continuous Recog practice?

It is a memory puzzle: determine if the currently shown image has appeared before in the sequence.
