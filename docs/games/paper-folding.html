Paper Folding

What will it look like when unfolded?

Play Paper FoldingRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap on the correct result grid out of the 4 options provided.

  1. Observe the folding sequence indicated by the arrows.
  2. Note where the hole punch is made on the folded paper.
  3. Mentally unfold the paper in reverse order.
  4. Select the option that matches your visualized hole pattern.

Strategy

  • Track the reflections of the punch across each fold line.
  • Eliminate options that have holes in impossible locations.
  • Remember that diagonal folds swap the X and Y coordinates.

What it asks of you

Determine the final pattern of holes after a folded piece of paper is punched and unfolded.
