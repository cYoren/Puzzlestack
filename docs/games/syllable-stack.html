Syllable Stack

Stack syllables to form complete words

Play Syllable StackRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the syllable blocks in the correct sequence to build a word. Tap the undo button to remove the last selected syllable.

  1. Look at the available syllables scattered on the screen.
  2. Try to identify familiar word beginnings or endings.
  3. Tap the syllables in order to construct a word in the active slots.
  4. Once a valid word is formed, it will lock in; continue until all syllables are used.

Strategy

  • Look for common prefixes (like 're-' or 'un-') or suffixes (like '-tion' or '-ing').
  • Sound out combinations in your head before selecting them.

What it asks of you

Combine the scattered syllables in the correct order to form complete, valid words.

Questions

Is Syllable Stack free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and no personal data collected. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Syllable Stack take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Syllable Stack?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Syllable Stack practice?

It is a word puzzle: combine the scattered syllables in the correct order to form complete, valid words.
