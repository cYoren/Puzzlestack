Boats

Place the fleet to match the row and column counts.

Play BoatsRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap a cell to cycle it through boat cell, water, and untouched. No dragging is needed.

  1. Read the numbers along the top and the left. A 0 empties that whole row or column, so tap every cell in it to water.
  2. Mark boat cells with the longest boat first. A run of four needs four cells in a line, so a line that cannot hold four anywhere is not part of it.
  3. Once a row or column has all the boat cells its count asks for, tap the rest of that line to water.
  4. Two boat cells that touch at a corner are a mistake even when both runs have the right length. Water one of them.
  5. The strip under the board crosses a boat off when you have marked a complete one. The board is finished when every boat is crossed off and the counts are met.

Strategy

  • Whenever a cell has boat cells on both a row neighbour and a column neighbour, it cannot be a boat: a boat is a straight run, so it would have to bend.
  • A boat cell rules out all four cells diagonally beside it, not just the four it touches.
  • If a line's count is only just satisfied by the cells still open, every one of them is a boat.
  • Count the fleet, not just the lines: when the remaining lengths cannot fit in a gap, that gap is water.

What it asks of you

Place the whole fleet: every boat is a straight run of boat cells, no two boats touch even at a corner, and the counts outside the grid say how many boat cells each row and column holds.

Questions

Is Boats free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Boats take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Boats?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Boats practice?

It is a logic puzzle: place the whole fleet: every boat is a straight run of boat cells, no two boats touch even at a corner, and the counts outside the grid say how many boat cells each row and column holds.
