Digit Swap Equations

Swap single digits between equations to correct both.

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Tap two digits to swap their positions and make the equation true.

  1. Examine the incorrect equation presented.
  2. Calculate the current results of the left and right sides.
  3. Identify which two digits, when swapped, will balance the equation.
  4. Tap the first digit, then tap the second digit to execute the swap.

Strategy

  • Look for the difference between the two sides to estimate what needs to change.
  • Focus on digits that have the largest impact (e.g., tens place vs ones place).
  • Check the operations; swapping numbers in a multiplication part changes the value drastically.

What it asks of you

Fix an incorrect mathematical equation by swapping two digits.
