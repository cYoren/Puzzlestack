Math Guess

Guess the hidden number within 15 chances.

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Use the on-screen keyboard to type an equation and tap submit.

  1. Enter a valid equation that equals the target number.
  2. Look at the color feedback (e.g., green for correct spot, yellow for wrong spot).
  3. Adjust your equation using the feedback.
  4. Find the exact hidden equation before you run out of tries.

Strategy

  • Start with an equation that uses diverse numbers and operations.
  • Remember order of operations (PEMDAS) applies to your guesses.

What it asks of you

Guess the hidden mathematical equation within the given number of tries, using color hints for correct numbers and operators.

Questions

Is Math Guess free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Math Guess take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Math Guess?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Math Guess practice?

It is a math puzzle: guess the hidden mathematical equation within the given number of tries, using color hints for correct numbers and operators.
