2048

Combine tiles with the same numbers to reach the 2048 tile.

Play 2048Runs in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Swipe up, down, left, or right anywhere on the screen to slide all tiles in that direction.

  1. Swipe to move all tiles in a single direction.
  2. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one tile with double the value.
  3. After every swipe, a new 2 or 4 tile appears on the board.
  4. Keep merging tiles to free up space and build towards 2048.

Strategy

  • Keep your highest value tile in one of the corners.
  • Restrict your swipes to primarily three directions to maintain your corner strategy.
  • Try to keep your tiles organized in a descending chain to allow for cascading merges.

What it asks of you

Combine tiles with the same numbers to reach the 2048 tile.

Questions

Is 2048 free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and no personal data collected. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one 2048 take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in 2048?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does 2048 practice?

It is a math puzzle: combine tiles with the same numbers to reach the 2048 tile.
