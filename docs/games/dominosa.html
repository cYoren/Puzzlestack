Dominosa

Pair adjacent numbers so each domino appears once.

Play DominosaRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap and drag between adjacent numbers to form a domino.

  1. Look at the grid of numbers.
  2. Identify pairs of adjacent numbers that make up valid domino tiles.
  3. Tap and drag to group two numbers together.
  4. Cover the entire grid exactly once with no overlapping dominoes.

Strategy

  • Look for pairs that can only be formed in one specific location.
  • Work from the edges and corners inward.
  • Keep track of which dominoes (e.g., 2-3, 4-4) you have already placed.

What it asks of you

Reconstruct a set of dominoes by pairing adjacent numbers on a grid.

Questions

Is Dominosa free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and no personal data collected. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Dominosa take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Dominosa?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Dominosa practice?

It is a logic puzzle: reconstruct a set of dominoes by pairing adjacent numbers on a grid.
