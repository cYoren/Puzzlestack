Fibonacci Merge

Merge consecutive Fibonacci numbers (1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13...).

Play Fibonacci MergeRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Swipe up, down, left, or right to move all tiles in that direction.

  1. Swipe the grid to move all tiles in the chosen direction.
  2. When two consecutive Fibonacci numbers (e.g., 2 and 3, or 5 and 8) collide, they merge into the next Fibonacci number (e.g., 5, 13).
  3. Note that 1s can merge with other 1s to make 2.
  4. Keep merging tiles to free up space and build larger numbers before the grid fills up.

Strategy

  • Keep your highest value tile in one specific corner of the grid.
  • Only swipe in two or three directions (e.g., Down and Right) to maintain organization.
  • Plan your moves ahead to bring consecutive numbers adjacent to each other.

What it asks of you

Slide tiles on a grid to merge consecutive Fibonacci numbers and reach the highest possible number.
