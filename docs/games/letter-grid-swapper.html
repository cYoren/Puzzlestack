Letter Swapper

Swap adjacent letter tiles to complete row and col words.

Play Letter SwapperRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap a tile to select it, then tap an adjacent tile to swap their positions.

  1. Look at the three TARGET WORDS displayed at the top.
  2. Find the letters for the first word and move them to the top row.
  3. Tap a tile, then tap an adjacent tile to swap them.
  4. Complete all three words horizontally to score a point and generate a new puzzle.

Strategy

  • Focus on forming one word at a time, starting from the top row.
  • Be careful not to mix up the letters of already completed words.

What it asks of you

Rearrange the letters in the 3x3 grid by swapping adjacent tiles to form the three target words horizontally.

Questions

Is Letter Swapper free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Letter Swapper take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Letter Swapper?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Letter Swapper practice?

It is a word puzzle: rearrange the letters in the 3x3 grid by swapping adjacent tiles to form the three target words horizontally.
