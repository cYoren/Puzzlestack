Block Escape

Slide blocks to clear a path to the exit.

Play Block EscapeRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Drag blocks along their horizontal or vertical axis to move them into empty spaces.

  1. Identify the distinct target block (usually colored differently) that needs to reach the exit on the right.
  2. Locate the blocks obstructing the target block's path to the exit.
  3. Drag the obstructing vertical and horizontal blocks out of the way to create a clear lane.
  4. Slide the target block through the exit to solve the puzzle.

Strategy

  • Work backwards: look at the exit and determine which block is directly blocking it, then figure out how to move that specific block.
  • Horizontal blocks can only move left/right, and vertical blocks can only move up/down.
  • Sometimes you have to move a block into the target's path temporarily to shuffle other blocks around.

What it asks of you

Clear a path to slide the target block out of the exit on the right side of the board.

Do I have to guess in Block Escape?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
