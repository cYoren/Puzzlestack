Same Game

Clear a group of two or more to score. Empty the board to win.

Play Same GameRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap a group of two or more connected squares of the same colour to remove it. The squares above fall and the columns close up.

  1. Look for the colour that covers the most board and start there.
  2. Remove groups that join two other groups of the same colour once they fall together.
  3. Keep the small groups for last; they are the ones that strand single squares.
  4. The board is cleared when no group of two or more is left.

Strategy

  • A single square can never be removed, so avoid cutting a group down to one.
  • Removing a tall group collapses the columns and can merge two groups that were apart.
  • Play from the bottom where you can: a collapse changes more of the board than a removal at the top.

What it asks of you

Clear the board by removing groups of two or more squares of the same colour.

Questions

Is Same Game free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Same Game take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Same Game?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Same Game practice?

It is a logic puzzle: clear the board by removing groups of two or more squares of the same colour.
