Sudoku

Complete the grid so that every row, column, and 3x3 box contains all digits 1 to 9.

Play SudokuRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap an empty cell on the grid to select it, then tap a number from the bottom keypad to fill it. Tap the selected cell again or the clear button to erase a number.

  1. Scan the grid for rows, columns, or 3x3 boxes that are almost full.
  2. Tap an empty cell in one of those areas.
  3. Determine the missing number and select it from the keypad.
  4. Repeat this process, using logic to deduce missing numbers until the entire grid is filled correctly.

Strategy

  • Start with the 3x3 boxes that have the most given numbers.
  • Look for naked singles where only one specific number can possibly fit in a cell based on its row, column, and box.

What it asks of you

Fill the 9x9 grid so that every row, column, and 3x3 box contains the digits 1 through 9 exactly once without any duplicates.

Questions

Is Sudoku free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Sudoku take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Sudoku?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Sudoku practice?

It is a logic puzzle: fill the 9x9 grid so that every row, column, and 3x3 box contains the digits 1 through 9 exactly once without any duplicates.
