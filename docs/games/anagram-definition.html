Anagram Definition

Solve the anagram based on its definition

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Tap the scrambled letters in the correct order to spell the word. Tap entered letters to remove them, or use the clear button.

  1. Read the provided definition carefully to understand the target word's meaning.
  2. Look at the scrambled letter tiles below the definition.
  3. Mentally rearrange the letters to find a word that fits the definition.
  4. Tap the letter tiles in order to spell the answer.
  5. If incorrect, clear your input and try a different arrangement.

Strategy

  • Look for common word prefixes (like 'un-', 're-') or suffixes (like '-ing', '-ed', '-tion') among the scrambled letters.
  • If you're stuck on the letters, focus entirely on the definition and think of synonyms.
  • Identify the vowels first and try placing them in typical patterns.

What it asks of you

Unscramble a given set of letters to form a word that matches the provided dictionary definition.
