Mirror Image

Identify the correct mirror reflection.

Play Mirror ImageRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the correct reflection from the list of multiple-choice options.

  1. Carefully observe the target image presented at the start.
  2. Examine the multiple-choice options presented.
  3. Mentally mirror the target image along the specified axis.
  4. Tap the option that matches your mental reflection to advance.

Strategy

  • Focus on asymmetrical details like protrusions or colors.
  • Pick one specific point on the target and find where it should end up after the flip.
  • Eliminate clearly incorrect options first to improve your odds.

What it asks of you

Identify the exact mirror reflection of the presented shape or image.
