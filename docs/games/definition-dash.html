Definition Dash

Match the correct word to its definition

Play Definition DashRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap on the definition button that corresponds to the meaning of the given word.

  1. Read the target word shown in the large colored box.
  2. Read through the four definition options provided below.
  3. Select the definition that correctly matches the word.
  4. If correct, you will advance; if incorrect, the game ends.

Strategy

  • Use root words and prefixes to deduce the meaning of unfamiliar words.
  • Eliminate obviously incorrect definitions first to narrow down your choices.
  • Pay attention to the part of speech (noun, verb, adjective) to match the definition style.

What it asks of you

Match the displayed vocabulary word with its correct definition.

Questions

Is Definition Dash free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Definition Dash take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Definition Dash?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Definition Dash practice?

It is a word puzzle: match the displayed vocabulary word with its correct definition.
