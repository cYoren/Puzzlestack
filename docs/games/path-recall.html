Path Recall

Memorize and repeat pathway selections.

Play Path RecallRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the grid cells in the correct sequence to reconstruct the path.

  1. Watch carefully as a path is highlighted on the grid.
  2. Wait for the path to disappear and the grid to clear.
  3. Tap the starting cell of the remembered path.
  4. Continue tapping adjacent cells to complete the sequence.

Strategy

  • Visualize the path as a distinct shape rather than individual steps.
  • Group the movements into chunks (e.g., 'two right, one up').
  • Keep your eyes on the grid even when it clears to maintain spatial memory.

What it asks of you

Remember a path shown briefly on a grid and recreate it exactly from memory.

Questions

Is Path Recall free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Path Recall take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Path Recall?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Path Recall practice?

It is a memory puzzle: remember a path shown briefly on a grid and recreate it exactly from memory.
