Room Inspector

Identify furniture modifications made while screen was blank.

Play Room InspectorRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the objects that were added, removed, or altered.

  1. Scan the room carefully during the observation phase.
  2. Memorize object positions, colors, and presence.
  3. Wait for the screen to blink or transition.
  4. Inspect the new room state and tap the discrepancy.

Strategy

  • Scan the room systematically (e.g., left to right, top to bottom).
  • Count the number of items on shelves or tables.
  • Focus on prominent objects that are likely candidates for change.

What it asks of you

Memorize the layout and objects in a room, then identify what changed after a brief transition.

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
