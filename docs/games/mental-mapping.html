Mental Mapping

Follow the directions and find the destination.

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Tap the final destination cell on the grid.

  1. Note your starting position on the grid.
  2. Read or listen to the sequence of directional movements (e.g., North, East).
  3. Track the movements mentally without touching the screen.
  4. Tap the final cell where the path ends.

Strategy

  • Cancel out opposing moves in your head (e.g., Up then Down means you didn't move).
  • Keep your eyes fixed on the starting point and move your focus exactly as instructed.

What it asks of you

Follow a series of directional instructions mentally and identify the final location on a map.

Questions

Is Mental Mapping free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Mental Mapping take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Mental Mapping?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Mental Mapping practice?

It is a spatial puzzle: follow a series of directional instructions mentally and identify the final location on a map.
