Affix Factory

Create valid words using prefixes and suffixes

Play Affix FactoryRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the correct affix button from the multiple-choice options provided.

  1. Read the base word displayed in the center of the screen.
  2. Look at the multiple-choice affix options (e.g., 'un-', '-ly', 're-').
  3. Determine which affix can be legally attached to the base word.
  4. Tap the correct affix to build the new word and progress to the next puzzle.

Strategy

  • Try saying the combined word out loud in your head to see if it sounds like a real word.
  • Pay attention to the hyphen position to know if you are choosing a prefix (before) or suffix (after).
  • Eliminate obviously incorrect affixes that break standard English spelling rules.

What it asks of you

Select the correct prefix or suffix that attaches to a given base word to form a new valid word.

Questions

Is Affix Factory free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and no personal data collected. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Affix Factory take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Affix Factory?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Affix Factory practice?

It is a word puzzle: select the correct prefix or suffix that attaches to a given base word to form a new valid word.
