Shikaku

Divide the grid into squares and rectangles matching the numbers.

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Drag across the grid cells to draw rectangles.

  1. Look at the numbers scattered across the grid.
  2. Drag to create a rectangle around a number.
  3. Ensure the area of the rectangle matches the number exactly.
  4. Fill the entire grid with non-overlapping rectangles.

Strategy

  • Start with large prime numbers, as they can only form 1xN or Nx1 rectangles.
  • Look for numbers trapped in corners or along the edges.
  • Use deduction: if a cell can only be reached by one specific number, include it in that rectangle.

What it asks of you

Divide the grid into rectangles such that each contains exactly one number, and its area equals that number.
