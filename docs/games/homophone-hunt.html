Homophone Hunt

Choose the correctly spelled homophone

Play Homophone HuntRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the button with the correctly spelled word.

  1. Read the sentence with the missing word.
  2. Review the multiple-choice options, which are homophones of each other.
  3. Determine which spelling fits the grammatical context and meaning of the sentence.
  4. Tap the correct option.

Strategy

  • Use context clues; e.g., 'their' shows possession, 'there' indicates a place, 'they'''re' is 'they are'.
  • If unsure, mentally substitute each definition into the sentence to see which makes logical sense.
  • Take your time to double-check the spelling, as the words are visually similar.

What it asks of you

Select the correct homophone (words that sound the same but have different meanings/spellings) to complete the sentence.

Questions

How long does one Homophone Hunt take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Homophone Hunt?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Homophone Hunt practice?

It is a word puzzle: select the correct homophone (words that sound the same but have different meanings/spellings) to complete the sentence.
