Rule Finder

Match each card by finding the hidden sorting rule.

Play Rule FinderRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap one of the four piles to place your card.

  1. Look at the card to be sorted at the bottom.
  2. Place it on one of the four reference piles.
  3. Receive correctness feedback. Use this to identify if the current sorting rule is shape, color, or number.
  4. Adapt your sorting to follow this rule until it changes again.

Strategy

  • Do not panic when a correct rule starts returning 'Incorrect' - it means the rule has shifted.
  • Always test rules systematically: color first, then shape, then number.
  • Remember the last correct rule to avoid repeating it immediately after a shift.

What it asks of you

Deduce the hidden rule for sorting cards (by color, shape, or number) based on correctness feedback, and adapt when the rule changes.

Questions

Is Rule Finder free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Rule Finder take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Rule Finder?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Rule Finder practice?

It is a logic puzzle: deduce the hidden rule for sorting cards (by color, shape, or number) based on correctness feedback, and adapt when the rule changes.
