Kakurasu Sums

Shade cells to meet weighted row/col index sums.

Play Kakurasu SumsRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap a cell to toggle its shaded state.

  1. Note the weights (1, 2, 3, 4...) located on the top and left edges of the grid.
  2. Check the target sums located on the right and bottom edges.
  3. Tap cells to shade them. A shaded cell in column 3 adds 3 to its row's sum.
  4. Continue shading until all row and column targets turn green.

Strategy

  • Look for high target numbers that require almost all cells in a row/column to be shaded.
  • Look for low targets (like 1 or 2) where only one specific cell can be shaded.
  • Use cross-referencing: shading a cell affects both its row and column sum simultaneously.

What it asks of you

Shade cells in a grid so that the sum of the weights of the shaded cells matches the target sums for each row and column.
