Modulo Clock

Calculate math equations using clocks.

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Tap the number buttons to input your calculated answer.

  1. Read the base modulo (e.g., Modulo 12, like a standard clock).
  2. Look at the given arithmetic expression.
  3. Perform the calculation and find the remainder when divided by the base.
  4. Tap the correct answer.

Strategy

  • For Modulo 12, just think of adding or subtracting hours on a clock face.
  • If a number is larger than the modulo, subtract the modulo until it's smaller.
  • Negative numbers mean counting backwards from the modulo base.

What it asks of you

Solve arithmetic problems using modular (clock) arithmetic.

Questions

Is Modulo Clock free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Modulo Clock take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Modulo Clock?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Modulo Clock practice?

It is a math puzzle: solve arithmetic problems using modular (clock) arithmetic.
