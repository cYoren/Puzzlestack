Crown

Place one crown in each row, column, and color region. Crowns cannot be adjacent, even diagonally.

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Tap empty cells to place or remove a crown. Long press to mark with an X.

  1. Analyze the colored regions on the grid.
  2. Place one crown in each region, row, and column.
  3. Ensure crowns do not touch even diagonally.
  4. Fill the necessary spots to solve the puzzle.

Strategy

  • Start with small regions where placement options are limited.
  • Use the X marker to cross out invalid cells.
  • Remember that corners diagonally adjacent to a crown are invalid.

What it asks of you

Place crowns on the grid so that no two share the same row, column, or colored region, and they don't touch diagonally.

Questions

Is Crown free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Crown take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Crown?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Crown practice?

It is a logic puzzle: place crowns on the grid so that no two share the same row, column, or colored region, and they don't touch diagonally.
