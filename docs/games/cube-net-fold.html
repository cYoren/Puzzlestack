Cube Net Fold

Select the matching 3d cube option

Play Cube Net FoldRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap on the correct 3D cube option (A, B, or C) at the bottom of the screen, then tap SUBMIT CHOICE.

  1. Observe the colors on the unfolded 2D cross-shaped net.
  2. Visualize how the net folds into a 3D cube.
  3. Examine the three 3D cube options provided.
  4. Select the cube that represents a valid rotation of the folded net and submit your choice.

Strategy

  • Look for opposite faces on the net (they will have one square between them) - they can never touch on the folded cube.
  • Track the relative orientation of three adjacent faces.
  • Eliminate options that show impossible color combinations.

What it asks of you

Identify which 3D cube configuration matches the unfolded 2D net shown.

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
