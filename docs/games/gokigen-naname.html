Gokigen Naname

Draw diagonals to connect points without loops.

Play Gokigen NanameRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap a cell to toggle the direction of its diagonal line (forward slash or backslash).

  1. Look at the numbered circles at the grid intersections.
  2. A number indicates exactly how many diagonal lines must connect to that circle.
  3. Tap cells to draw diagonals, ensuring they satisfy all numbered intersections.
  4. Check that your lines do not create any closed loops.

Strategy

  • Start with corners and edges; a '0' or '4' in the middle, or '0' or '2' on an edge, forces specific diagonals.
  • If a '1' is in a corner, only one line can connect to it.
  • Remember that loops are forbidden; if placing a line would close a loop, you must use the opposite diagonal.

What it asks of you

Draw a diagonal line in every cell so that the lines meeting at each intersection match the number clue, without forming any closed loops.
