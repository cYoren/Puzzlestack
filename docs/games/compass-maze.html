Compass Maze

Navigate a maze where movement directions change based on your compass orientation.

Play Compass MazeRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap an adjacent tile in a valid direction to move.

  1. Look at the compass arrows on your current tile.
  2. Move to an adjacent tile that aligns with one of those arrows.
  3. Repeat to find a path to the goal.
  4. Reach the goal tile to win.

Strategy

  • Work backwards from the goal to see which tiles point to it.
  • Avoid tiles with arrows that point to dead ends or boundaries.
  • Look for loops and avoid getting trapped.

What it asks of you

Navigate a grid where you can only move in the directions indicated by the current tile's compass.
