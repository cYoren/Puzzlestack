Sdmt Test

Use the key above to find the digit for the symbol shown.

Play Sdmt TestRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the number on the numpad that corresponds to the displayed symbol.

  1. Review the key at the top pairing symbols with digits 1-9.
  2. Look at the large target symbol presented in the center.
  3. Find its corresponding digit in the key.
  4. Tap the matching digit on the keypad as quickly as possible.

Strategy

  • Memorize the most frequent symbol-digit pairs to save time.
  • Keep your eyes on the target symbol and key, and rely on muscle memory for the keypad.
  • Maintain a steady rhythm to maximize your score.

What it asks of you

Match the correct digit to the presented symbol based on the provided key within the time limit.
