Yajilin

Place black cells and draw a single loop matching clues.

Play YajilinRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap cells to draw loop segments; double tap to toggle black shading.

  1. Check arrow clues indicating the exact number of black cells in that direction.
  2. Ensure no two black cells touch orthogonally.
  3. Draw a continuous single loop visiting all remaining empty white cells.

Strategy

  • Clue cells cannot be visited by the loop and cannot be shaded black.
  • If a cell is shaded black, the loop must travel around it.

What it asks of you

Draw a single closed loop through unshaded cells and shade cells black according to directional arrow clue counts without adjacent black cells.

Do I have to guess in Yajilin?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
