Mirror Tracing

Trace the star! Controls are MIRRORED.

Play Mirror TracingRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Drag your finger across the screen to draw; your strokes will appear mirrored.

  1. Look at the dotted outline of the shape you need to trace.
  2. Place your finger on the starting point.
  3. Drag your finger to trace the shape, adjusting for the inverted movement.
  4. Complete the full outline with minimal deviation to achieve a high score.

Strategy

  • Move slowly and deliberately, especially around corners.
  • Focus your eyes on the drawing tip rather than your finger.
  • Anticipate the opposite direction before making a move.

What it asks of you

Trace a given shape as accurately as possible while your input is mirrored.
