Subitize Rush

Instantly identify the quantity of flashed dots without counting.

Play Subitize RushRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the matching number button.

  1. Focus your gaze on the center of the canvas.
  2. When the dots flash, capture the pattern as a holistic visual group.
  3. Select the exact number of dots you observed.

Strategy

  • Group dots mentally into small clusters of 2, 3, or 4.
  • Do not count one-by-one; rely on quick visual perception.

What it asks of you

Instantly identify the quantity of briefly flashed dots without sequential counting.
