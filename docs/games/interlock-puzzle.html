Interlock Puzzle

Disassemble or assemble complex interlocking blocks in the correct sequence.

Play Interlock PuzzleRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Drag on the screen to rotate the 3D shapes. Tap 'YES' or 'NO' to submit your answer.

  1. Examine 'Shape A' and 'Shape B'.
  2. Drag your finger across the screen to rotate both shapes and view them from all angles.
  3. Mentally rotate one shape and try to slide it into the empty spaces of the other.
  4. Tap 'YES' if they interlock perfectly, or 'NO' if they do not.

Strategy

  • Count the number of blocks in both shapes; their sum must equal the total volume of the target solid.
  • Look for prominent protrusions on one shape and find the corresponding indentations on the other.
  • Rotate the shapes to align their axes mentally, making it easier to visualize the fit.

What it asks of you

Determine if two 3D block shapes will fit together perfectly to form a solid cube/prism.
