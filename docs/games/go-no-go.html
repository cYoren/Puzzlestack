Go / No-Go

Tap for Green, IGNORE Red!

Play Go / No-GoRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap anywhere on the screen for 'Go'. Refrain from tapping for 'No Go'.

  1. Wait for a symbol to appear on the screen.
  2. If the symbol is a 'Go' (green/play icon), tap the screen as fast as possible.
  3. If the symbol is a 'No Go' (red/cross icon), do nothing and wait for it to disappear.
  4. Build your score by correctly reacting to a series of symbols.

Strategy

  • Keep your finger hovering near the screen but avoid resting it to prevent accidental taps.
  • Focus primarily on the color or shape to make split-second decisions.
  • Stay relaxed; over-anticipating can cause false starts on 'No Go' symbols.

What it asks of you

Quickly tap the screen when a 'Go' symbol appears, and do NOT tap when a 'No Go' symbol appears.

Questions

Is Go / No-Go free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Go / No-Go take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Go / No-Go?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Go / No-Go practice?

It is a attention puzzle: quickly tap the screen when a 'Go' symbol appears, and do NOT tap when a 'No Go' symbol appears.
