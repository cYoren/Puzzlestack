Symmetry

Mirror the left pattern onto the right grid.

Play SymmetryRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap or drag across the empty cells on the right side of the grid to toggle them on or off to match the reflection.

  1. Observe the pattern of filled cells on the left side of the grid.
  2. Imagine a vertical mirror line down the center of the grid.
  3. Tap the cells on the right side to fill them in the mirrored positions.
  4. Ensure the right side is an exact reflection of the left side to complete the puzzle.

Strategy

  • Work column by column, starting from the center mirror line and moving outwards.
  • Count the empty spaces from the center line to accurately place mirrored cells.

What it asks of you

Complete the symmetrical pattern by toggling cells on the right half of the grid so that it perfectly mirrors the pre-filled left half.
