Palindrome Builder

Create a palindrome by adding minimal letters

Play Palindrome BuilderRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap or drag letters into the correct slots to form the word.

  1. Review the available letter tiles.
  2. Identify pairs of identical letters, as they must mirror each other.
  3. Place the single, unpaired letter (if any) in the exact center.
  4. Arrange the remaining pairs symmetrically to spell a valid word.

Strategy

  • Always find the center letter first if the word length is odd.
  • Work from the outside in, placing common prefixes and their mirrored suffixes.
  • Ensure the resulting string actually spells a real word in the dictionary.

What it asks of you

Construct a valid palindrome (a word that reads the same forwards and backwards) using given letter tiles.

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
