Target 10

Select a combination of number tiles from the 5x5 grid whose sum equals exactly 10.

Play Target 10Runs in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the tiles on the grid to select or deselect them and add their values to your current selection sum.

  1. Look at the numbers available on the 5x5 grid.
  2. Tap a number to start your selection.
  3. Tap additional numbers, keeping an eye on the Selection Sum indicator.
  4. Select a combination that adds up to exactly 10 to clear those tiles and score points.

Strategy

  • Look for obvious pairs that make 10, like 9+1 or 8+2.
  • You can use three or more numbers (e.g., 5+3+2) if simple pairs are not available.

What it asks of you

Select a combination of number tiles from the 5x5 grid whose sum equals exactly 10.
