Divided Attention

Tap LEFT for STAR ⭐ | Tap RIGHT for RED 🔴

Play Divided AttentionRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Use left-side controls for one task and right-side controls for the other.

  1. Understand the requirements of Task A (e.g., sorting shapes).
  2. Understand the requirements of Task B (e.g., dodging obstacles).
  3. Monitor both sides of the screen continuously.
  4. React to stimuli on both sides to keep the game going.

Strategy

  • Do not fixate on one side of the screen; keep your eyes moving back and forth.
  • Prioritize the task that has an immediate impending failure condition.
  • Rely on peripheral vision to detect movement or changes.

What it asks of you

Perform two different tasks simultaneously without letting either fail.
