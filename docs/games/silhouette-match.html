Silhouette Match

Which silhouette matches this object?

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Tap the correct silhouette from the available options.

  1. Observe the detailed reference image carefully.
  2. Look at the provided silhouette options.
  3. Notice subtle differences in posture, edges, or negative space.
  4. Select the silhouette that is an exact outline of the reference.

Strategy

  • Focus on the edges and negative spaces rather than the center.
  • Look for small, distinctive features like a raised finger, hair strand, or uneven edge.
  • Eliminate silhouettes that clearly lack a prominent feature of the original.

What it asks of you

Find the exact silhouette that matches the detailed image.
