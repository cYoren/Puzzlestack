Silent Letter Search

Identify silent letters in the given words

Play Silent Letter SearchRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the word with a silent letter, or tap the specific letter itself.

  1. Analyze the presented word or list of words.
  2. Pronounce the words in your head to check phonetic spelling.
  3. Identify the letter that is written but not pronounced.
  4. Make your selection before the time runs out.

Strategy

  • Watch out for common silent letter combinations like 'kn', 'wr', 'mb', and 'gh'.
  • Remember that 'e' at the end of a word is often silent and changes the vowel sound.
  • Sound out the word carefully to ensure every letter is accounted for.

What it asks of you

Identify words containing silent letters or pinpoint the specific silent letter within a word.
