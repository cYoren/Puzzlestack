Area & Perimeter

Draw rectangles matching target area and perimeter values.

Play Area & PerimeterRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the '+' and '-' buttons on the sides of the rectangle to increase or decrease its width and height.

  1. Note the Target Area (Width × Height) and Target Perimeter (2 × (Width + Height)).
  2. Observe the current dimensions of the rectangle on the screen.
  3. Use the '+' and '-' controls to change the width and height.
  4. Find the specific pair of factors that multiply to the target area AND add up to half the target perimeter.
  5. The game completes the round automatically when the correct dimensions are reached.

Strategy

  • Focus on the Area first by finding factor pairs (e.g., if Area is 24, pairs are 1x24, 2x12, 3x8, 4x6).
  • Once you have a factor pair, check if its perimeter matches the target.
  • A square (or near-square) minimizes perimeter for a given area, while a long, thin rectangle maximizes it.

What it asks of you

Adjust the dimensions of a rectangle until its area and perimeter match the given target values.
