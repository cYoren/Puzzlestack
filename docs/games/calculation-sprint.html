Calculation sprint

Solve as many equations as possible in 60 seconds.

Play Calculation sprintRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the correct answer from the 2x2 grid of multiple-choice options.

  1. Tap start to begin the countdown timer.
  2. Read the math equation presented in the center of the screen.
  3. Quickly calculate the answer.
  4. Tap the matching option from the choices below.
  5. Answer correctly to increase your score and reach the target goal before time expires.

Strategy

  • Don'''t overthink; rely on your quick mental math reflexes.
  • Use estimation or look at the last digit of the numbers to quickly eliminate wrong multiple-choice options.
  • Maintain a steady rhythm and don'''t panic if you make a mistake.

What it asks of you

Solve as many math equations as possible before the time runs out.

Questions

Is Calculation sprint free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Calculation sprint take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Calculation sprint?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Calculation sprint practice?

It is a math puzzle: solve as many math equations as possible before the time runs out.
