Action Recall

Repeat character gesture animation sequences in order.

Play Action RecallRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the corresponding action buttons to replay the sequence.

  1. Observe: Watch the character execute a sequence of animation gestures (e.g. waving, jumping, bowing).
  2. Memorize: Mentally log each gesture as it happens, creating verbal shorthand names for each (e.g. 'W-J-B').
  3. Interact: When the sequence finishes and inputs unlock, tap the action buttons in the exact same order.

Strategy

  • Say the actions out loud or repeat them in a rhythm in your head.
  • Assign simple visual shorthand tags to each movement.
  • Don't click prematurely; wait for the full sequence to complete before starting input.

What it asks of you

Observe a sequence of character gesture animations and repeat them in the exact order they were shown.
