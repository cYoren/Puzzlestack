KenKen

Fill 1- without repeating in rows/cols

Play KenKenRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap a cell to select it, then tap a number on the numpad. Use the backspace icon to clear.

  1. Look at the cages (heavily outlined areas) and their target clues (e.g., '3+', '12x').
  2. Find single-cell cages, which require no math and give you free numbers.
  3. Tap empty cells and input numbers that satisfy the cage's math operation.
  4. Ensure no number repeats in any row or column.

Strategy

  • Start with single-cell cages and cages with only one possible number combination (e.g., '3+' in a 2-cell cage must be 1 and 2).
  • Remember that subtraction and division cages can have numbers in any order (e.g., a '2÷' cage could be 4 and 2, or 2 and 4).
  • Use Sudoku-style row/column elimination to determine the exact placement of numbers within a cage.

What it asks of you

Fill the grid with numbers 1 to N (grid size) without repeating in any row or column, such that the numbers in each outlined 'cage' achieve the target value using the specified math operation.

Questions

Is KenKen free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one KenKen take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in KenKen?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does KenKen practice?

It is a math puzzle: fill the grid with numbers 1 to N (grid size) without repeating in any row or column, such that the numbers in each outlined 'cage' achieve the target value using the specified math operation.
