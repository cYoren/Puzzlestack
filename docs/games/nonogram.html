Nonogram

Reveal the hidden image using logic clues.

Play NonogramRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap a cell to fill it, tap again to mark it as empty (X), or long-press to drag.

  1. Look at the numbers at the beginning of each row and column. They indicate consecutive filled cells.
  2. Use intersecting clues to deduce which cells must be filled.
  3. Mark cells you know must be empty with an 'X' to narrow down possibilities.
  4. Continue deducing until the entire grid is correctly filled.

Strategy

  • Start with the largest numbers, especially those that take up more than half the row/column.
  • Always mark empty spaces with an 'X' once you complete a clue block.
  • Never guess; every move should be based on pure logic.

What it asks of you

Fill in a grid based on numerical clues to reveal a hidden pixel art picture.

Questions

Is Nonogram free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and no personal data collected. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Nonogram take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Nonogram?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Nonogram practice?

It is a logic puzzle: fill in a grid based on numerical clues to reveal a hidden pixel art picture.
