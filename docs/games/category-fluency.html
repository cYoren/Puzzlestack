Category Fluency

Enter items belonging to the category

Play Category FluencyRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Use the keyboard to type a word into the text field, then tap the send icon or press submit on the keyboard.

  1. Read the category prompt (e.g., '''Animals''', '''Fruits''') at the top of the screen.
  2. Tap the text input field to open your keyboard.
  3. Think of a word that fits the category, type it, and submit it.
  4. Valid words will be accepted and added to your list as chips.
  5. Reach the target word count before the timer hits zero to win.

Strategy

  • Think in sub-categories (e.g., for '''Animals''', think of pets, then farm animals, then zoo animals) to jog your memory.
  • Type quickly and don'''t worry about entering complex words; simple, common words score just the same.
  • If you get stuck, look around your immediate environment or think through the alphabet for inspiration.

What it asks of you

Type as many words as possible that fit the given category before the time runs out.

Questions

Is Category Fluency free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Category Fluency take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Category Fluency?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Category Fluency practice?

It is a word puzzle: type as many words as possible that fit the given category before the time runs out.
