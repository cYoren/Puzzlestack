Shape Shifter

Tap the target shape-color combination.

Play Shape ShifterRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the correct final shape from the given options.

  1. Memorize the initial shape, color, and orientation.
  2. Watch the sequence of transformation rules applied (e.g., rotate 90°, invert color).
  3. Mentally apply these transformations step-by-step.
  4. Select the final outcome from the choices.

Strategy

  • Update your mental image of the shape after every single transformation.
  • Pay attention to the order of operations, as it affects the final state.
  • Use your fingers to trace the rotation if visualization gets difficult.

What it asks of you

Track a shape as it undergoes various transformations and identify the final result.

Questions

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Shape Shifter?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
