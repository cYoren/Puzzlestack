Pangram Sprint

Build a sentence using every letter of the alphabet

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Type the missing letters or drag words into order.

  1. Read the incomplete sentence provided on screen.
  2. Determine which letters of the alphabet have not yet been used.
  3. Deduce the missing word that uses those exact remaining letters.
  4. Input the correct word to complete the pangram.

Strategy

  • Keep a mental checklist of rare letters like Q, X, Z, and J.
  • Use grammar and context clues to figure out what type of word is missing.
  • Remember classic pangrams like 'The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog'.

What it asks of you

Identify missing letters or assemble a pangram (a sentence containing every letter of the alphabet).

Questions

Is Pangram Sprint free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Pangram Sprint take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Pangram Sprint?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Pangram Sprint practice?

It is a word puzzle: identify missing letters or assemble a pangram (a sentence containing every letter of the alphabet).
