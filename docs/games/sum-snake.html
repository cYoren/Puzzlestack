Sum Snake

Connect adjacent numbers to reach the target sum.

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Tap and drag across adjacent grid cells (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) to draw your path. Release to submit.

  1. Note the target sum required at the top of the screen.
  2. Find a starting number on the grid.
  3. Drag your finger across adjacent numbers, keeping track of the running sum.
  4. Release your finger once the sum of the highlighted path matches the target exactly.

Strategy

  • If the target is large, look for paths containing high numbers.
  • If you overshoot the target, retrace your path backwards to deselect cells.

What it asks of you

Trace a continuous, non-overlapping path through the grid of numbers so that the sum of the numbers on the path exactly equals the target value.
