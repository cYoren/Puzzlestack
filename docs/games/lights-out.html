Lights Out

Tap tiles to flip neighbours until all lights go out.

Play Lights OutRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap a tile to toggle it and its immediate neighbors (up, down, left, right).

  1. Observe the initial pattern of lit and unlit tiles.
  2. Tap a tile to turn it off, noting how it flips its neighbors.
  3. Work your way across the grid to systematically turn off lights.
  4. Achieve a completely dark board to win.

Strategy

  • Try solving row by row, turning off all lights in the current row by clicking the row below it.
  • Use patterns to solve the final row if you get stuck.

What it asks of you

Turn off all the lights on the grid. Tapping a light toggles its state and the state of adjacent lights.

Questions

Is Lights Out free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and no personal data collected. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Lights Out take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Lights Out?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Lights Out practice?

It is a logic puzzle: turn off all the lights on the grid. Tapping a light toggles its state and the state of adjacent lights.
