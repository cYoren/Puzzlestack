Block Stacking

Stack irregular block panels balancing gravity weight.

Play Block StackingRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap anywhere on the screen to drop the currently moving active block.

  1. Watch the active block slide back and forth across the top of the stack.
  2. Time your tap so the active block aligns perfectly with the block below it.
  3. When you tap, the block drops. Any overhanging portion is sliced off, making your next block smaller.
  4. Continue stacking until you reach the target height to win.
  5. If you miss the stack entirely, the tower falls and the game is over.

Strategy

  • Focus your eyes on the edges of the stationary block below, and tap exactly when the moving block's edge aligns with it.
  • As the blocks get smaller, the game gets harder; prioritize perfect alignment early on.
  • Get into a rhythm, as the block speed increases slightly with each successful drop.

What it asks of you

Stack moving blocks on top of each other as high as possible by timing your drops perfectly.
