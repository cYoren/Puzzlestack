Einstein riddle

Use the clues to deduce the attributes of each house.

Play Einstein riddleRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap grid cells to mark them as true (check) or false (cross) based on the clues.

  1. Read the list of available logical clues.
  2. Cross-reference attributes on the logic grid.
  3. Tap a cell to mark an intersection as true (which auto-crosses out the rest of the row/column).
  4. Continue deducing until the entire grid is correctly filled.

Strategy

  • Start with the most direct clues that explicitly link two items.
  • Use negative clues to eliminate possibilities immediately.
  • Look for chain reactions where placing one checkmark forces another.

What it asks of you

Use a set of clues to deduce the attributes belonging to different subjects on a grid.
