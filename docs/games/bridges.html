Bridges

Connect islands with bridges. Each island needs a specific number of bridges. Bridges cannot cross.

Play BridgesRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap an island to select it, then tap an adjacent island to draw a bridge. Tap again to upgrade to a double bridge, or tap a third time to remove the connection.

  1. Review the numbered islands on the grid.
  2. Tap an island to select it, then tap an orthogonal (up, down, left, right) island to connect them with a bridge.
  3. Bridges cannot cross other bridges or islands.
  4. Each island must have a total number of connected bridges equal to its number.
  5. All islands must be connected together in one single group to solve the puzzle.

Strategy

  • Look for corners with '4', edges with '6', or center islands with '8'. These require double bridges in all available directions.
  • If a '1' island is connected, no other bridges can attach to it.
  • Ensure you don'''t create an isolated closed loop of islands; the network must be fully connected.

What it asks of you

Connect all islands into a single continuous network with bridges, ensuring each island has exactly the number of bridges indicated by its number.
