Gear Rotation

Predict the rotation direction of the last gear.

Play Gear RotationRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the 'CLOCKWISE' or 'COUNTER-CLOCKWISE' button.

  1. Observe the chain of interconnected gears.
  2. Note the rotation direction of the first gear (indicated by an arrow).
  3. Trace the rotation through the chain, remembering that adjacent gears rotate in opposite directions.
  4. Select the correct rotation direction for the final gear.

Strategy

  • Adjacent gears always rotate in opposite directions.
  • If there is an odd number of gears in the chain, the first and last gears rotate in the same direction.
  • If there is an even number of gears, the first and last gears rotate in opposite directions.

What it asks of you

Determine the rotation direction of the final gear in a sequence, given the starting gear's direction.
