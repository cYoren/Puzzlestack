Counting Sheep

How many sheep did you see?

Play Counting SheepRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap a difficulty level to start. Watch the sheep pass, then use the keyboard to type the number and tap 'SUBMIT ANSWER'.

  1. Select a difficulty level (Easy, Medium, or Hard) to start the game.
  2. Pay close attention to the screen and count the sheep emojis as they move across from left to right.
  3. When all the sheep have finished passing and the input screen appears, type the total number you counted into the text field.
  4. Tap the 'SUBMIT ANSWER' button to verify your count.

Strategy

  • Keep your eyes focused slightly off-center to see the whole screen and avoid missing fast-moving sheep.
  • If sheep appear in clusters, try grouping them in small sets (like 2s or 3s) mentally instead of counting by ones.

What it asks of you

The objective is to accurately count the number of sheep that move across the screen and submit the correct total.
