Event Timeline

Arrange historical/fictional event cards chronologically.

Play Event TimelineRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Drag and drop the event cards into the correct chronological sequence.

  1. Study the list of events and their corresponding dates or timestamps.
  2. Once the study phase ends, the events will be shuffled and dates hidden.
  3. Drag the events up and down to arrange them from earliest to latest.
  4. Submit your timeline to check your accuracy.

Strategy

  • Create a mental story connecting the events in the order they occurred.
  • Focus on anchoring the first and last events securely in your memory.
  • Look for logical cause-and-effect relationships between the events.

What it asks of you

Memorize a sequence of historical or fictitious events and their dates, then place them in chronological order.
