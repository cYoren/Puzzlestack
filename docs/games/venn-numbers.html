Venn Numbers

Categorize numbers into the correct Venn diagram regions.

Play Venn NumbersRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Drag and drop the number tokens into the Venn diagram sections.

  1. Read the property tags for each circle (e.g. 'Prime', 'Even', 'Multiple of 3').
  2. Analyze the properties of the given number token.
  3. Drag the number into the intersection where it satisfies all matching circle properties.
  4. Place all numbers correctly to win.

Strategy

  • Check if the number fits none of the categories and belongs on the outer boundary.
  • Take your time validating overlapping circle criteria.
  • Find numbers that fit the central intersection of all circles first.

What it asks of you

Place numbers into the correct intersections of a Venn diagram according to numerical properties.
