Fillomino

Group cells into regions matching their number.

Play FillominoRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap cells to cycle through numbers from 1 to 9.

  1. Examine the starting numbers on the grid.
  2. Each number represents the size of the block (polyomino) it belongs to.
  3. Tap adjacent cells to assign them the same number to group them.
  4. Ensure no two blocks of the same size share a horizontal or vertical edge.
  5. Fill all grid cells to solve the puzzle.

Strategy

  • Start with small numbers like 1, which are immediately isolated blocks.
  • Look for paths that are forced to extend because they cannot merge with adjacent groups of the same size.
  • Keep track of block sizes by counting cells with the same number.

What it asks of you

Divide the grid into polyominoes of matching sizes. Two polyominoes of the same size cannot touch share an edge.

Questions

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and no personal data collected. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Fillomino take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Fillomino?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Fillomino practice?

It is a logic puzzle: divide the grid into polyominoes of matching sizes. Two polyominoes of the same size cannot touch share an edge.
