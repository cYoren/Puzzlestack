Attentional Blink

Spark rapid targets flashing in streams.

Play Attentional BlinkRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Use the keyboard to type the first and second target numbers you spotted, then submit.

  1. Fixate your eyes on the center of the screen as a rapid stream of letters flashes by.
  2. Stay alert and look for the first number (Target 1) hidden among the letters.
  3. Immediately prepare to spot the second number (Target 2), which will appear shortly after.
  4. Once the sequence ends, type both numbers in the order you saw them.

Strategy

  • Do not move your eyes. Keep a soft focus on the exact spot where the characters appear.
  • Beware of the 'attentional blink'—a split second after spotting the first number, your brain might miss the second one if it appears too quickly. Force yourself to stay alert.
  • Say the first number out loud the moment you see it to free up your visual attention for the second number.

What it asks of you

Identify the two numbers that briefly appear within a rapid, continuous sequence of letters.
