Sentence Unscramble

Reorder words to form a valid sentence

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Drag and drop the words into the correct order, or tap them sequentially.

  1. Read all the jumbled words provided on the screen.
  2. Identify the subject, verb, and object of the sentence.
  3. Arrange the words in proper syntactic order.
  4. Submit the completed sentence to verify correctness.

Strategy

  • Look for capitalized words to start the sentence and punctuation to end it.
  • Group common phrases or compound nouns together first.
  • Read your formed sentence aloud in your head to check for natural flow.

What it asks of you

Rearrange the jumbled words to form a grammatically correct and meaningful sentence.

Questions

Is Sentence Unscramble free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Sentence Unscramble take?

About a minute. Dealt from the deck it uses the size a first-timer finishes in under two minutes; the full sizes are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Sentence Unscramble?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Sentence Unscramble practice?

It is a word puzzle: rearrange the jumbled words to form a grammatically correct and meaningful sentence.
