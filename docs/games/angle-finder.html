Angle Finder

Calculate the missing angle in the geometric problem.

Play Angle FinderRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Drag the slider at the bottom of the screen to adjust the angle of the movable arm.

  1. Look at the target angle value (e.g., 45°, 120°) displayed on the screen.
  2. Visualize what that angle looks like relative to a 90° right angle or 180° straight line.
  3. Drag the slider to rotate the dynamic arm until the visual angle matches your estimate.
  4. Tap the 'Submit' button to lock in your guess.
  5. Aim for high precision to maximize your score over multiple rounds.

Strategy

  • Use benchmark angles: 90° is a perfect L-shape, 180° is flat, 45° is exactly halfway between 0 and 90.
  • If the target is 135°, think of it as 90° plus another 45°.
  • Make small, fine adjustments using the slider before submitting your final answer.

What it asks of you

Estimate and recreate a target angle by adjusting a movable protractor arm.
