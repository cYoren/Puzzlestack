Spelling Sprint

Race against the clock to spell as many words as you can.

Play Spelling SprintRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Swipe or tap the appropriate button for 'Correct' or 'Incorrect'.

  1. A word flashes on the screen.
  2. Quickly analyze its spelling.
  3. Decide if it is a valid spelling or contains a typo.
  4. Make your choice before the timer runs out.

Strategy

  • Trust your immediate visual recognition; correctly spelled words often 'look' right.
  • Watch out for common tricky pairs like 'ie' vs 'ei' and double consonants.
  • Don't overthink it, as the time limit is strict.

What it asks of you

Rapidly identify whether the presented word is spelled correctly or incorrectly.

Questions

Is Spelling Sprint free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Spelling Sprint take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Spelling Sprint?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Spelling Sprint practice?

It is a word puzzle: rapidly identify whether the presented word is spelled correctly or incorrectly.
