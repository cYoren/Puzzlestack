Fraction Match

Match the visual to its fraction

Play Fraction MatchRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the matching fraction cards or drop them into matching slots.

  1. Look at the fraction representation shown (visual pie or numerical).
  2. Find the matching card with the equivalent value (e.g., 2/4 matches 1/2).
  3. Tap or drag to connect them.
  4. Match all cards correctly to complete the level.

Strategy

  • Simplify fractions to their lowest terms immediately.
  • For visual pies, count the total segments first (denominator) and then shaded parts (numerator).
  • Practice common fraction conversions (halves, quarters, thirds).

What it asks of you

Match equivalent fractions or match fraction values to their visual representation.

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Fraction Match?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
