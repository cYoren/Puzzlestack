Exponent Tower

Order dynamic power expressions from smallest to largest.

Play Exponent TowerRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap available power blocks to add them to your tower; tap placed blocks to remove them.

  1. Calculate the numerical value of each base raised to its exponent.
  2. Tap the smallest power first to form the base of the tower.
  3. Stack subsequent powers upward in strict ascending numerical order.

Strategy

  • Remember key powers of 2 (e.g. 2^5 = 32, 2^6 = 64) and 3 (3^3 = 27, 3^4 = 81).
  • Compare same bases by their exponents directly.

What it asks of you

Calculate powers and stack exponential expressions in ascending order from smallest to largest.

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
