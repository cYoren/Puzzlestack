Context Clues

Pick the word that best fits the sentence.

Play Context CluesRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Select the best word from the multiple-choice options.

  1. Read the sentence with the blank space.
  2. Analyze the context and tone of the sentence.
  3. Review the options.
  4. Tap the word that best completes the thought.

Strategy

  • Look for signal words (like '''however''' or '''because''').
  • Eliminate options that are grammatically incorrect.
  • Read the completed sentence to see if it makes logical sense.

What it asks of you

Determine the meaning of a missing word based on the surrounding sentence context.

Questions

Is Context Clues free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Context Clues take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Context Clues?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Context Clues practice?

It is a word puzzle: determine the meaning of a missing word based on the surrounding sentence context.
