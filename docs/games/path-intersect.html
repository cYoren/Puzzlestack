Path Intersect

Navigate multiple paths simultaneously to reach their respective targets without collision.

Play Path IntersectRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

Tap the specific cell on the grid where the paths cross.

  1. Observe the two different colored paths drawn on the screen.
  2. Follow the trajectories of both paths visually.
  3. Locate the single cell where they overlap.
  4. Tap the intersection cell to verify your answer.

Strategy

  • Focus on the general region where horizontal and vertical segments meet.
  • Trace one path with your eyes and look for the color break of the other.
  • Don't rush; confirm the grid square aligns with both path lines.

What it asks of you

Identify the exact grid coordinate where two overlapping colored paths intersect.
