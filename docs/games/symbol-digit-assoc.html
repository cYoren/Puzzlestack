Symbol-Digit Assoc

Solve symbol translation sheets matching digit keys.

Play Symbol-Digit AssocRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

How to play

During the recall phase, tap a symbol box to select it, then tap the number pad to input the corresponding digit. Tap the clear button to erase an entry, and submit when finished.

  1. Study the association key showing which symbol equals which digit.
  2. Tap Start Translating to hide the key and begin the test.
  3. Look at the challenge symbols provided.
  4. Use the keypad to enter the correct digit for each symbol from memory and submit.

Strategy

  • Create visual stories linking the shape of the symbol to the number.
  • Focus on memorizing 3 or 4 pairs at a time rather than all 9 at once.

What it asks of you

Memorize the association between 9 unique symbols and the digits 1-9, then correctly translate a sequence of challenge symbols into their corresponding digits using a keypad.

Do I have to guess in Symbol-Digit Assoc?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.
