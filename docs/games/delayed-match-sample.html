Delayed Match

Match target symbol after solving math distractors.

Get PuzzlestackThe 1.1.0 release is finishing. The source is public today.

How to play

Observe the sample, wait for the delay to end, and tap the matching option.

  1. A sample image or pattern will be shown briefly on the screen.
  2. The screen will go blank or show a distraction for a delay period.
  3. Several options will appear.
  4. Tap the option that perfectly matches the original sample.

Strategy

  • Focus on specific unique features or colors in the sample rather than the whole image.
  • Mentally repeat the visual features during the delay period.
  • Do not let the blank screen break your concentration.

What it asks of you

Remember a target pattern or image, wait through a delay, and then identify it among distractors.

Questions

Is Delayed Match free?

Yes. Puzzlestack is free to download, with no advertising, no account and nothing collected about you. Every puzzle can be played at least once.

How long does one Delayed Match take?

A few minutes at the size it opens; bigger boards are in the library.

Do I have to guess in Delayed Match?

No. Where a puzzle has one answer, the generator proves it before dealing, and the clues are enough to get there by reasoning.

What does Delayed Match practice?

It is a memory puzzle: remember a target pattern or image, wait through a delay, and then identify it among distractors.
