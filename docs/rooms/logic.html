Logic puzzles

Deduction with one answer. The grid, the loop and the numbers tell you everything you need, and none of it needs a guess.

Logic is the room this app was built around. Most of these come from the Tatham and Nikoli canon: one solution, every clue earning its place, no step that requires guessing. Start with Nonogram or Sudoku if you have never done one; Slitherlink and Masyu are the same idea with a line.
