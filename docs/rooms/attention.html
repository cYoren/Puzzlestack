Attention puzzles

Find the thing, ignore the noise, and keep it up for a minute.

Attention games give you one rule and a board that argues with it. Tap the colour, not the word. Find the one that does not match. They are the fastest cards in the app and the ones people replay most.

Play a puzzleRuns in your browser, nothing to install.

Every attention puzzles puzzle (5)

  • Change BlindnessSpot the changing item
  • Color matchAdjust the sliders to match the target color as closely as possible.
  • Schulte TableTap numbers from one to twenty-five.
  • Shell GameTrack a hidden ball as the shuffling cups swap places.
  • Visual SearchFind the unique symbol in the grid
