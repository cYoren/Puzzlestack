Spatial puzzles

Turn it, fold it, walk it. Shapes that change when you are not looking.

Spatial puzzles ask you to hold an object in your head and move it. Some are mazes with a strange camera, some are nets that fold into a cube, some are silhouettes to match. Paper Folding is the classic.
