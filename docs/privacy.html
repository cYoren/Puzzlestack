Privacy policy for Puzzlestack

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Puzzlestack is a collection of offline brain puzzles. It does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

What Puzzlestack stores

Puzzlestack stores a small amount of data locally on your device only, so the app can remember your progress between sessions:

  • A nickname you choose during onboarding (you may enter anything, including a random one the app suggests).
  • Your experience points, level and total puzzles solved.
  • Per-puzzle streaks and the dates you last solved them.
  • Your favourite puzzles and app settings, such as theme and trial mode.

This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else, because Puzzlestack has no server to upload it to.

What Puzzlestack does not do

  • It does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • It does not contain advertising, advertising identifiers or ad SDKs.
  • It does not contain analytics, telemetry or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not require an account or an internet connection to play.

Deleting your data

All Puzzlestack data is held in the app's own private storage. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage through your device's settings, permanently deletes everything listed above. No copy is retained anywhere else.

Links to other sites

Some screens link out to external pages, such as the project's source repository on GitHub or its store listing. Opening one hands off to your browser or store app, and those services have their own privacy policies. This policy covers only Puzzlestack itself.

Children's privacy

Puzzlestack is suitable for general audiences. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Puzzlestack changes what it stores or introduces any network communication, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
