What it is not

No feed to scroll, no streak to protect, no energy to spend. A puzzle arrives, you solve it, the next one is dealt. If you want to choose instead, the whole catalogue is one tap away and filed by room.

Every puzzle here is played in the app, and everything the app is made of is public: the source, the full list, and the rules for each card written on its page.
