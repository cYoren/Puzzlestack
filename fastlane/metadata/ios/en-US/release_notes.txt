Puzzlestack 1.1.0 is the first store release. The feed is now the front door: open the app and a puzzle is already on screen. Swipe up and the next card is dealt.

- 184 puzzles across six rooms, dealt one card at a time in a steady rhythm.
- Daily Five: five puzzles for the day, the same for everyone, with a streak and a plain text result to share.
- The Stack: pick a game, its board size and its difficulty, and the app remembers your choice.
- Puzzlestack Plus: one optional purchase, paid once, for unlimited picks, hints and the Daily Five archive.
- Every board reads and plays with a screen reader. No ads, no account, no sign in.
