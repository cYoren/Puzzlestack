Puzzlestack is a feed of real puzzles. Open it and you are already playing: swipe up and the next one is dealt.

One good puzzle, then another. Each card is fair, each one is made to be finished, and none of them is filler. 230 puzzles across six rooms: logic, math, memory, spatial, attention and word.

THE DEAL
- A vertical feed of puzzle cards. Swipe up for the next one, or stay on the one that has you.
- The same puzzles in six rooms when you want to choose instead of be dealt to: Logic, Math, Memory, Spatial, Attention, Word.
- Every board works with one finger; the newer ones take a drag as well.
- Offline. No ads. No account. Nothing tracked.

WHY IT FEELS DIFFERENT
- One card, one screen, one decision. The rules are a tap away, never a wall of text before the first move.
- Nothing is a timer where a puzzle should be thought about.
- Your own times and your streak, against yourself only. No leaderboards, no invented percentiles.
