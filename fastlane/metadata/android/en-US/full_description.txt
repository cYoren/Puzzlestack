Puzzlestack is a vertical-feed suite of 313 minimalist brain puzzles: logic, math, memory, spatial, attention and word games, in one calm app.

THREE WAYS IN
- Shuffle: a full-screen vertical feed. Swipe up and drop straight into a fresh puzzle.
- Daily Pick: one puzzle a day, the same for everyone.
- The catalog: 313 games in six rooms (Logic 53, Math 63, Memory 54, Spatial 38, Attention 54, Word 51).

WHY IT FEELS DIFFERENT
- A discovery home instead of a wall of 313 icons: a Shuffle hero, a Daily Pick, quick games under two minutes, and the full catalog tucked into six rooms.
- Every board works with one finger, and the newer ones let you drag as well.
- Offline. No ads. No account. Nothing tracked.

HIGHLIGHTS
Sudoku, Nonogram, Slitherlink, Kakuro, Minesweeper, Water Sort, 2048, KenKen, Dominosa, Skyscrapers, Futoshiki, Magnets, Tents & Trees, Galaxies, Signpost, N-Back, Corsi Blocks, Schulte Table, Stroop, Crossword, Word Search, Word Ladder.
